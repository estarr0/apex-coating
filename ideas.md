## Chosen Approach: Industrial Luxury — Forged Precision

### Design Movement
Industrial Luxury / Corporate Premium — the visual language of high-end industrial brands meeting consumer-friendly accessibility.

### Core Principles
1. **Navy as Anchor**: Deep corporate navy (#0A1B3D) grounds every section, providing authority and trust.
2. **White as Luxury**: Generous pristine white space creates breathing room and premium feel.
3. **Swatch-Driven Accents**: Paint colors themselves become the accent palette — dynamic, vibrant, tied to BS 4800 swatches.
4. **Sleek Depth**: Soft shadows, subtle gradients, and glassmorphism on overlays create dimension without heaviness.

### Color Philosophy
- **Primary**: Deep corporate navy `#0A1B3D` — authority, trust, industrial heritage
- **Secondary**: Modern slate grey `#475569` — sophistication, neutrality
- **Background**: Pristine white `#FFFFFF` and off-white `#F8FAFC` — clarity, premium
- **Accent**: Vibrant paint-swatch colors (dynamic, from BS 4800 palette) — energy, product showcase
- **CTA**: Electric blue `#2563EB` — action, conversion

### Layout Paradigm
Asymmetric hero with full-width carousel, grid-based product catalog with card depth, side-drawer cart, and full-bleed shade card grid. Sections flow with generous vertical rhythm.

### Signature Elements
1. **Paint Swatch Grid**: High-density color grid as a visual centerpiece
2. **Glassmorphic Overlays**: Frosted glass effect on carousel CTAs and cart drawer
3. **Industrial Detail Lines**: Thin accent lines and technical labels (BS codes, hex values) as design motifs

### Interaction Philosophy
Every interaction feels tactile — swatches respond to hover with full-color preview, paint buckets tilt on hover, cart drawer slides with spring physics. The site feels like a premium paint showroom.

### Animation
- Hero carousel: smooth 500ms transitions with Ken Burns effect
- Swatch hover: 200ms scale + shadow lift
- Cart drawer: 300ms spring slide-in
- Product cards: 180ms lift on hover with shadow expansion
- Scroll reveals: 400ms fade-up with stagger

### Typography System
- **Display/Headings**: "Sora" — geometric, bold, industrial precision (weights 600-800)
- **Body**: "DM Sans" — clean, readable, modern (weights 400-500)
- **Technical/Code**: "JetBrains Mono" — for BS codes, hex values, technical labels

### Brand Essence
Apex Coating East Africa Ltd is the premium coatings authority for East Africa, delivering industrial-grade quality with consumer-friendly accessibility. Professional, trustworthy, vibrant.

**Personality adjectives**: Authoritative, Precise, Vibrant

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and confident. CTAs are action-oriented. Microcopy is technical but accessible.

Example headlines:
- "Color Engineered for East Africa"
- "Every Shade. Every Surface. Every Standard."

Example CTAs:
- "Explore Our Palette"
- "Get a Custom Quote"

### Wordmark & Logo
A bold geometric "APEX" wordmark with a paint-drop accent integrated into the "A" — symbolizing precision and the product. The logo uses navy with a single accent color drop.

### Signature Brand Color
Deep Corporate Navy `#0A1B3D` — unmistakably Apex.
